# Scoring Rubric

Five criteria, scored 1–5, weighted to a total out of 100. Every score cites the evidence behind it.

---

## Weights and Formula

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Fit | 25% |
| Timing | 25% |
| Commercial potential | 20% |
| Evidence quality | 15% |
| Access | 15% |

**Total** = (Fit × 25 + Timing × 25 + Commercial × 20 + Evidence × 15 + Access × 15) ÷ 5

A business scoring 5 on everything totals 100. A business scoring 1 on everything totals 20.

**Worked example:** Fit 4, Timing 5, Commercial 3, Evidence 4, Access 3
= (100 + 125 + 60 + 60 + 45) ÷ 5 = **78, Pursue** (no gates hit)

## Score Anchors

### Fit (25%)
| Score | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 5 | Priority 1–3 segment in the context file's target list, in a priority market, with assets the services build on directly (heritage, signature product, strong identity) |
| 4 | A listed segment and market, with a clear service match |
| 3 | A listed segment, but a weaker service match or a lower-priority segment |
| 2 | An edge-of-profile business (for example, a national chain outlet) or a weak service match |
| 1 | Outside the ideal client list, or hits an anti-persona |

### Timing (25%)
| Score | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 5 | A dated trigger 2–9 months out, confirmed by a Tier 1 or 2 source |
| 4 | A dated trigger 1–2 or 9–12 months out, or a credible trigger with an approximate date |
| 3 | A credible but undated trigger (for example, a reported expansion with no date), or a trigger less than 1 month out where a later phase or follow-on moment is still open |
| 2 | A weak trigger (generic season, more than 12 months away, or already passed), or a dated trigger less than 1 month out with no sign that planning is still open |
| 1 | No trigger found |

### Commercial potential (20%)
| Score | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 5 | Signs of budget and scale (multiple locations, an investor-backed launch, major partners) and room for repeat work |
| 4 | An established business with a clear project worth doing, likely able to pay for it |
| 3 | A plausible single project, with budget unclear |
| 2 | Small or fragile operation; likely below the studio's minimum project size |
| 1 | No realistic paid project, or the only fit is free or equity-only work |

Base this on observed signals (locations, partners, press, scale), not guesses about revenue.

### Evidence quality (15%)
| Score | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 5 | All material claims from Tier 1 or 2 sources less than 6 months old; contested facts resolved |
| 4 | Material claims well sourced, with minor gaps |
| 3 | Mixed: some material claims rest on a single or older source |
| 2 | Key claims rest on Tier 3 sources or on hypotheses |
| 1 | Mostly unsourced |

**Single-source trigger cap:** if the trigger date rests on one source, Evidence can't exceed 3 unless that source is Tier 1 (the business's own site or announcement, an official record, or the owner's own words quoted in press). See [evidence-standards.md](evidence-standards.md) for the tiers.

**Skipped research isn't a cap.** Skipping customer-experience research, or being unable to read social channels, doesn't lower Evidence by itself. It must be reported in the Research Coverage table, and outreach can't use findings from any step that was skipped or unavailable.

### Access (15%)

Score Access from the matrix below. Confidence levels and route rules are in [decision-makers-and-contacts.md](decision-makers-and-contacts.md).

**Access matrix** (kept identical in `SKILL.md`, `references/scoring-rubric.md` and `references/decision-makers-and-contacts.md`)

Person, the best one found:
- **A:** a named decision-maker, high confidence
- **B:** a named decision-maker, medium confidence, or a named champion (GM, marketing, events or partnerships lead)
- **C:** no one named, or only a low-confidence name

Route, the best one published:
- **Direct written:** a confirmed warm introduction, or an email published for that named person or their role
- **General written:** a general business inbox (info@, hello@), a contact or inquiry form on the business's own site (including a third-party form the site itself links to), or the business's own social inbox, used manually
- **Phone or walk-in only:** the business's published phone number, or an in-person visit in a market the user can realistically visit
- **None:** no published route

**Unverified routes.** Score Access only on routes verified in this run. A route that couldn't be found or read scores as None. The prospect stays in the scored ranking on that score. It isn't dropped, and its score isn't raised on assumption.

| Person / Route | Direct written | General written | Phone or walk-in only | None |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | 5 | 4 | 3 | 1 |
| **B** | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 |
| **C** | — | 2 | 2 | 1 |

**Hard gates:** Access 2 or lower can't be Pursue. Access 1 means there is no usable route.

**Pursue vs. Ready to Contact.** Pursue is a tier: 75 or more, with no gate hit. A Pursue prospect is **Ready to Contact** only when its best route is written (Direct or General written) the user can see and use that address or form now, and the original-source check of its outreach claims is complete. A Pursue prospect reachable only by phone or walk-in, or whose published email is obfuscated and not yet viewed by the user, is Pursue but not Ready to Contact. Its required next action is to call or visit (or view the address on the site) to get a written route. A call or visit is for obtaining the route, not for pitching unexpectedly. Any draft for it is provisional and carries the label **Do not send until a written route is confirmed.**

## Tiers

| Tier | Total | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Pursue** | 75+ (and no gate hit) | Opportunity and outreach drafts now. Send only when Ready to Contact (see the Access matrix). |
| **Nurture** | 55–74 | Add to the ledger with the date to revisit, or the verification that would lift it |
| **Watch** | 40–54 | Log the trigger to watch for; no outreach |
| **Skip** | Below 40, or a Skip gate | Record why, and move on |

**Qualified** (for the Mode A "5 best-qualified" rule) means Pursue or Nurture: a total of 55 or more with no Skip gate. List Watch candidates separately, below the shortlist, and never count them toward the five.

**Pursue means outreach is justified now.** Every Pursue prospect has Access 3 or higher. Whether a draft can be sent today depends on Ready to Contact. If a prospect would score 75+ but has Access 2 or lower, it's Nurture with the next action "find a route" or "find the owner's name."

**Ranking and the shortlist cut.** Rank qualified prospects by total score first. Tie-breakers apply only when two totals are exactly equal, in this order: Pursue and Ready to Contact, then Pursue, then Nurture; then higher Timing; then higher Evidence; then higher Access. After the cut, show the first candidate below it, with its score, tier and the reason it missed. Never round a total up to cross a tier line or the cut.

**A shortlist is complete only when every finalist is scored.** If any finalist is unscored, label the shortlist Provisional, name the unscored finalists, and say why the run stopped. Report fully scored qualified prospects, Watch candidates, and unscored finalists as separate groups.

---

## Gates (override the total)

| Condition | Result |
|---|---|
| Timing ≤ 2 | Can't be Pursue (max Nurture) |
| Evidence ≤ 2 | Can't be Pursue until verified |
| Access ≤ 2 | Can't be Pursue (max Nurture until a qualifying name and route are found) |
| The only visible need is a do-not-pitch service | Skip |
| Current client, already pitched, in the ledger, or a competitor of a current client | Skip (or route to the user's judgment) |
| Permanently closed, closing, or in visible distress with no credible recovery path (see SKILL.md Step 1, reason 1) | Skip |

## Presenting Scores

```
| Business | Fit | Timing | Comm. | Evid. | Access | Total | Tier | Gate |
| [Name] | 4 — [why, source] | 5 — [trigger, date, source] | 3 — [why] | 4 — [why] | 3 — [route] | 78 | Pursue | — |
```

Never present a score without its reason. Don't round a total up to cross a tier line.
